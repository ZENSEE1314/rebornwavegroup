Short white-label launch video for Bridge X (an all-in-one platform for venues, sold under the merchant's own brand). Reborn Wave is the case study, NOT the product name.

## Output
- Composition directory: composition/
- Rendered video: ../brag.mp4
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: ~23s

- Strongest claim: "Your venue. Your brand. One platform."

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: app-store
- Creative direction: premium white-label SaaS launch for venues
- Angle: one platform runs the whole venue, under your brand; end on 2 pricing options + contact.
- Hook: gold→purple "Bridge X" wordmark + "Your venue. Your brand. One platform."
- Outro: "Text +65 8910 8241 to know more."
- Avoid: generic SaaS filler, abstract washes, chaos, unreadable fast text.

- Text: #ffffff and rgba(255,255,255,0.62)

- Body font: system-ui / Inter

## Audio
- Audio role: warm corporate bed
- Music: assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 at ~0.32, gentle fade under CTA
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset for vol-1; lock the logo (Scene 1) and CTA (Scene 6) near strong cues; snap the 4 feature cards to consecutive beats (hold the set after)

- Audio files: copy chosen music + SFX into composition/assets/

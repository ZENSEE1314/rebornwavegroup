# Brag Plan: Bridge X

## What is this app?
Bridge X is a white-label, all-in-one platform for nightlife & hospitality businesses — a branded member app (rewards, games, bookings, karaoke), a full POS with inventory, built-in accounting, and a WhatsApp CRM bot that auto-replies, signs up members and wins customers back. One system, your brand.

## The angle
"Run your whole venue on one platform — under your own brand." Show the real product surfaces (member app, POS, WhatsApp bot, accounting) as clean feature cards, then land the two pricing options and the contact number. Confident B2B product launch, not a joke.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen → gold/purple "Bridge X" wordmark snaps in with the line **"Your venue. Your brand. One platform."** — premium, immediately signals white-label.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Member app card: rewards, pet game, spin, karaoke (KOS), bookings — "Keep guests coming back."
- POS + Inventory card: table tickets, stock by category, cash drawer + receipts.
- Accounting card: income, expense, profit, commissions — "Books that keep themselves."
- WhatsApp Auto-CRM card: auto-reply in EN/中文/Bahasa, auto-create members, win-back reminders.

## Outro / punchline
Two pricing options (Monthly / One-time) → big CTA: **"Text +65 8910 8241 to know more."**

## User flow worth showing
none — this is a white-label pitch; centerpiece is recreated product feature cards (real surfaces: member dashboard tiles, POS, WhatsApp bot, accounting dashboard) rather than a single click-through flow.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: premium white-label SaaS launch for venues
- Interpretation: clean feature-card reveals, smooth slides, confident holds; every line readable; gold-on-dark premium restraint, no chaos.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 23s

## Audio direction
- Role: warm corporate bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (upbeat, app-store fit)
- Music treatment: start 0s at 0.32, gentle fade-out under the final CTA
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset for vol-1; target 1-2 strong cues for the logo hook and the CTA payoff; snap the 4 feature cards to the beat grid
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; gold logo/CTA glow breathes with RMS. No waveform/EQ visuals.
- SFX posture: moderate; card slide/drop per feature card, one soft bell on the CTA
- Audio-coupled moments: feature cards arrive one by one (card SFX); CTA payoff (soft bell)
- Restraint rule: no strobing, no aggressive hits; keep it premium

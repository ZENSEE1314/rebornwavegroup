## Create and approve
- [ ] Application received or company created in the BridgeX console
- [ ] Approved; owner copied in as the company's main admin
- [ ] Data space made (schema `tenant_<id>`); no Reborn starter content copied

## Brand
- [ ] Name, logo (512 px), tab icon, share picture
- [ ] Design, palette or own colours, font
- [ ] Pet name and pictures (if the pet is used)
- [ ] Custom domain (if any)

## Functions
- [ ] App functions switched on / off (BridgeX › App functions)
- [ ] Industries listed (BridgeX › App functions › Industries of …)

## Business set-up (the company's own admin)
- [ ] Tables and booking areas
- [ ] Tax and service fee, receipt details (name, logo, footer, QR)
- [ ] Payroll rules checked against the country's current rates
- [ ] Staff and managers created with roles; admins created from the console
- [ ] Products, stock and suppliers loaded (Excel import); bundles, packages, happy hour
- [ ] Channels: WhatsApp (its own number), Messenger / Instagram, Telegram (as needed)
- [ ] Hardware (receipt printer, kitchen screen, customer screen, TV, karaoke bridge) as needed

## Test day
- [ ] Order, kitchen Ready and Collected, pay by each method, print, refund, close the day
- [ ] Accounting and payroll read correctly
- [ ] Nothing of Reborn shows in screens, receipts, messages or e-mails
- [ ] Isolation: the company cannot see Reborn data and Reborn cannot see its data

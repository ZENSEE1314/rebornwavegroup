Questions

Answers before you ask.

What can you automate?

Repetitive work across sales, operations and support: lead follow-up, bookings and reminders, order taking, stock and purchasing, payroll hours, reporting and customer replies on WhatsApp.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You describe how the work happens today; we build, deploy and train your team. You get an admin console, not a codebase to maintain.

Can it carry my own brand?

Yes. Your name, logo, colours, domain and WhatsApp number. Customers only ever see you, and your data is isolated from every other business.

How long does it take?

A white-label launch is typically days to a few weeks. Custom products are scoped in the first call, with a timeline you agree to before we start.

Does it work with the tools I already use?

Usually. We connect through official APIs — WhatsApp Business, payment gateways, email and more — and tell you up front if something cannot be integrated.
